THE GAMES

The Sealed Universe is an anthology of investigative horror. You are never given a weapon. You are given a voice recorder, a camera, a notebook — and a place that has been waiting a very long time for someone who knows how to listen.

Every game is built on the same conviction: the scariest thing in any room is the truth nobody wrote down. The walls remember. The record is incomplete. You are the witness.

Each entry runs free in your browser — no install, no downloads. First-person, headphones recommended, roughly self-contained, quietly connected.

THE SERIES

GAME ONE — AVAILABLE NOW

THE WEEPING WALLS

Mara Voss told the truth for a living, and it cost her everything. Six months in a rented Victorian manor was supposed to be a place to disappear. But Ashford Manor selects its tenants. The walls breathe — five seconds in, five seconds out. The west wing door is locked, and hinged on the wrong side. And at 2:47 AM, the recorder she left running captures a woman's voice with every consonant stripped away.

The house is the antagonist. Not a ghost. Not a monster. The house itself — its architecture, its memory, its need to be witnessed.
